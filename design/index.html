Design Direction

Reading the jungle, rationalizing the irrational.

Design that starts in nature. Biomimicry and green principles set the direction, then the system makes it buildable. Nine projects, nine ideas read from the living world.

Biomimicry
Learn structural and organizational logic from living systems, then rationalize it into buildable form.
Green design
Orientation, daylight, passive comfort, and low-carbon materials as first-order constraints, not finishing touches.
Living within
The site's climate and ecology lead the parti, so a building lives within its environment rather than sits upon it.

Nine ideas, read from nature

Each square is a project. Flip it to see the concept behind the design.

Ritual + climateDaheli Fude Temple*Flip ⟳
ConceptA Daoist temple study built around complex curved wall geometry, where the challenge was rationalizing the curves into a coherent, buildable, fully editable system. The difficulty was holding continuity, offsets, wall thickness, and clean joins, with a well-resolved construction set drawn straight from the parametric logic.
Façade + lightMy Museum*Flip ⟳
ConceptA museum façade study controlling an envelope of 2,000+ trapezoidal glass panels (40 x 60 cm), each angled differently. The challenge was holding that variation as a legible, buildable rule set rather than manual exceptions, with panel angles scripted from an Excel value table and the full construction set produced through BIM in Revit.
Structure + growthCrouching Dragon*Flip ⟳
ConceptA tubular bridge driven by a single base curve, the structure, openings, and detailing set by one repeatable parametric system. The challenge was computational: holding continuity across an organic, high-curvature geometry while cutting tubular windows and threading an overhead span, all kept editable end to end in Dynamo with early AI-assisted form shaping.
Biomimicry + passiveCalla Lily SchoolFlip ⟳
ConceptA biomimicry-led school in Ethiopia where the form of the native calla lily sets the geometry of the classrooms, roof, and circulation, and reshapes the conventional teacher-facing room into clustered focus groups. Eight seven-meter rings pinwheel from a central point to rationalize structure and cost, while north-facing glazing, shaft ventilation, and two integrated tanks handle daylight, airflow, and water collection.
Site + habitatNorth Fork HouseFlip ⟳
ConceptA North Fork ranch house and pavilion study where multiple design options were developed against real site and greenbelt conditions. The work stayed grounded in architectural practice: resolving program, entry sequence, and landscape as a documented, buildable system rather than a single generated image.
Energy + ecologyHsinta Eco Power plantFlip ⟳
ConceptHsinta is a closed-loop power plant concept where bio-diesel generates electricity and the exhaust feeds rooftop agriculture, which in turn cycles back into the fuel supply. It was my first true generative design project, scripting around 1,000 roof panels in Dynamo to turn that closed-loop idea into a single coherent, buildable roof system.
Filter + ecologyBiomimicry Mercury FilterFlip ⟳
ConceptAn award-winning biomimicry study that reads mercury filtration from living systems — the blue-dot logic turned into a water-treatment proposition where natural remediation principles set the form, performance targets, and buildable assembly.
Desert + careJeddah HospitalFlip ⟳
ConceptA large hospital campus where desert climate, clinical program, and circulation are held as one ruled system — massing, shading, and orientation respond to heat and care pathways rather than treating each as a separate design pass.
Network + towerNetwork TowerFlip ⟳
ConceptA vertical structure driven by network logic — connectivity, load paths, and repetition rationalized into a tower where the frame reads as a single coordinated system rather than stacked independent elements.

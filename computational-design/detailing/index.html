Why nodes carry the design

A building fails at its weakest node. The concept lives or dies at the points where assemblies actually connect — parapet, sill, threshold. Each node is where intent meets gravity, water, and tolerance; resolve one poorly and the whole graph reads the failure.

Where headless AI struggles

A model can generate one striking image. It cannot hold the gradual variance across hundreds of coordinated nodes, where each point shifts slightly with substrate, angle, and tolerance, yet must stay consistent with the whole graph. That accumulated, cross-referenced judgement is exactly what construction documents demand and what generation alone cannot keep honest.

Rows of nodes are the proof

Renders persuade; nodes legitimize. A wall of resolved points in the graph is evidence that the idea was carried all the way to buildable, not stopped at the picture. This is the part of practice that earns trust on site, and the part a headed workflow protects.

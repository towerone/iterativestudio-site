About

A studio built on iteration.

Iterative Studio is an architecture and computational design practice working between Taiwan and the US. We pair traditional, licensed architectural practice with a headed AI workflow: AI works underneath the architect to test, refine, and optimize, while every decision stays in human hands. The result is buildings that live within their environment, and documents you can actually build from.

The Studio

Architecture that starts with the environment and ends in buildable documents.

What we design

  • Zero-energy and sustainable homes
  • Multi-unit residential and interior renovations
  • Community structures and pavilions
  • Parametric facades and computational systems

How we work

  • Environment first: site, climate, and ecology shape the concept
  • Computational design: editable geometry and readable parameters
  • Headed AI: AI explores options, the architect decides

What you receive

  • BIM models from concept through construction documents
  • Details resolved for real construction, not just renderings
  • Cost savings found through AI-assisted optimization

Where we work

  • Based between Taiwan and the US
  • Zero-energy homes delivered in Colorado
  • Residential and interior work delivered in Taiwan

The Founder

An architect who builds the tools as well as the buildings.

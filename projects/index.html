Research · Robotics · 01
Actively building
Selene: a street-level environmental sensing robot
Satellites see city heat in broad strokes. They miss what happens under trees, between buildings, at the height where people actually live. Selene is a mobile sensor robot built to gather that missing street-level data and feed it into a generative AI model that predicts how heat moves through urban space.
  • 3D printed structure, with parts developed through generative design in Rhino and Grasshopper
  • Dry-bulb, wet-bulb, and globe temperature sensing on a remote-controlled chassis with onboard processing
  • Each printed version tests structure, weight, and sensor placement; the design is iterating right now
Professional practice · 02
Ongoing
Manufacturing R&D: one mold instead of five
Ongoing R&D inside a precision manufacturing environment, where the studio's generative design methods move from architecture into product tooling. Client details are held in confidence.
  • Unified product tooling: one mold now serves a family of products that previously required five
  • Python generative design used to analyze materials, structural performance, and production workflows
  • Current focus: process automation and AI integration across the manufacturing line
Architecture · 03
In design
Rio Rancho house: headed AI meets conventional framing
A house used as a working testbed for one question: can AI tools be introduced into an ordinary, conventional framing workflow without taking the architect's hands off the wheel? AI works underneath, testing framing and detailing variables, while the documents stay in Revit.
  • Conventional wood framing kept as the base method; nothing exotic
  • AI explores dimensional and detailing variables to find real cost windows, such as trimming a span to cut material cost by up to 25 percent
  • Every AI suggestion is verified in the headed model before it touches the drawings
